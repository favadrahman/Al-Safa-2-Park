Executive Summary

Al Safa Park 2 is reimagined as an advanced, self-sustaining urban ecosystem, establishing a new benchmark in municipal design.

Driven by AI spatial and microclimate optimization, the dual-realm design delivers a fully operational, year-round civic space tailored to overcome regional climate extremes.

An open-air ecological landscape integrates seamlessly with a climate-sheltered smart loop, guaranteeing continuous community programming regardless of seasonal weather conditions.

Project Vision

The vision permanently eliminates the limitations of seasonal climate extremes. A 100% solar-powered, climate-controlled elevated Infinity-Loop guarantees uninterrupted access to wellness, fitness, and culture 365 days a year.

This continuous architectural ribbon serves as an iconic landmark, housing universally accessible, multi-functional community zones for all demographics.

Design Approach

Absolute energy autonomy is achieved through a rooftop Building-Integrated Photovoltaic (BIPV) grid, powering the entire facility while generating a net-positive surplus.

Thermal stability is guaranteed by pairing demand-controlled HVAC with triple-silver Low-E glazing. This envelope delivers a Visible Light Transmittance (VLT) ≥ 60%, a Solar Heat Gain Coefficient (SHGC) ≤ 0.15, and a U-Value ≤ 1.0 W/m²K—blocking 85% of solar radiation without sacrificing panoramic views.

Ground-level site planning actively regulates the microclimate. A 30% water body allocation, vertical green walls, and high-albedo surfaces work synergistically to reduce extreme summer ground temperatures by up to 3°C.

Spatial Philosophy _ Ground-Level Park Zone

An open-air ecological foundation dedicated to organic landscape recreation and natural microclimate regulation.

Zones feature plaza pools, meandering pathways, running tracks, family pavilions, sitting benches, and cooling vertical green walls.

Programmed for open-air running, family gatherings, wellness walks, and outdoor relaxation amidst native regional flora.

Dual-Realm Strategy _ The Upper Infinite Smart Loop Park

A suspended, climate-controlled architectural ribbon providing intensive, all-season public programming protected from extreme weather.

Partitioned into an adult gym, indoor game zone, interactive kids' play area, exhibition spaces, and continuous indoor walkways.

Ensures structured physical fitness, interactive gaming, and cultural exhibitions operate continuously, entirely independent of external climate conditions.

Alignment with Municipal Objectives

Fully aligns with Dubai Municipality’s core pillars of identity, human-centricity, ecosystem, and seasonality through distinctive entrances, multi-level walkability, and adaptive climate management.

Destination Playscapes & Edutainment: Interactive pavilions and creative kids' zones.

Active Recreation: Adult fitness gym, indoor game zones, running tracks, and walking paths.

Passive Recreation: Family picnic pavilions, shaded benches, plaza pools, and open lawns.

Special Recreation & Services: Exhibition zones, cultural amphitheaters, and essential amenities.

Commercial & Wayfinding: Food and craft markets integrated with IoT digital routing.

Target Audience: Dedicated multi-generational zones explicitly serve families, sports enthusiasts, teens, and pet owners.

Peak Visitor Capacity: The dual-level spatial framework doubles the functional footprint, easily exceeding the 150–400 peak visitor requirement without overcrowding during 1–3 hour dwell times.

Events & Zoning: Staggered functional zoning draws visitors inward. The layout supports seasonal celebrations, community competitions, and art installations, achieving top-tier ratings for accessibility, walkability, and thermal comfort.
